WHO IS HEread at low resolution, like everything else here

i was supposed to get a second pass. the artist went to lunch somewhere around 1998 and the build shipped without him, so everything you are looking at right now is the placeholder that was never meant to leave the office.

my draw distance is two meters. past that there is fog, and inside the fog there is nothing that i am responsible for. this arrangement has kept me calmer than most animals of my size.

i have 214 triangles in total and six of them went into the eyes. the eyes were the priority, the eyes are still the priority, and i will defend that budget decision to anyone who brings it up.

people keep asking when the update arrives. the disc is sealed, the studio is gone, the source files are on a drive nobody can find — and i am, frankly, completely fine with the way this turned out.

RULE 01 · STAY ON LOW

high settings are for dogs with something to hide behind their textures. i have nothing to hide, because out there past the second meter there is nothing built to hide behind.

RULE 02 · DON'T LOOK PAST THE FOG

the fog is not keeping a secret from you. the fog is politely covering the part of the world where the level designer stopped working, and it has been doing that job beautifully since launch day.

RULE 03 · THE EYES STAY

you can take my shadows, my mipmaps, my second row of teeth and both of my ears if the frame budget demands it. the eyes are load-bearing and the eyes are not negotiable.

RULE 04 · NEVER REQUEST THE UPDATE

every time somebody drags the quality slider upward i lose a hind leg for roughly four seconds and the floor turns into a texture from a different game. i would genuinely rather you didn't.
